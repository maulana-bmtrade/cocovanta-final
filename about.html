About COCOVANTA

Focused Indonesian charcoal sourcing and export coordination.

COCOVANTA is an independent trading and sourcing business based in Central Java, Indonesia. Our current website focus is coconut-shell charcoal briquettes for international BBQ and shisha buyers. We work with supplier networks and coordinate buyer requirements, product review and export preparation.
