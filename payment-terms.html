Commercial and payment terms

Quotation validity

Any quotation will state its own validity period, product description, quantity, currency, price basis and other applicable conditions. No price or availability is guaranteed until confirmed in a written offer.

Product and lot confirmation

Product photos and catalogue figures are indicative. Actual feedstock, dimensions, performance, packaging, production availability and test evidence must be confirmed for the specific supplier lot before an order is accepted.

Incoterms and logistics

The applicable Incoterms® rule, named place or port, loading arrangements, freight responsibilities and shipment schedule must be agreed in writing for each transaction.

Payment

Payment method, deposit, balance schedule, beneficiary details and due dates are transaction-specific and will be stated in the accepted proforma invoice or sales contract. Verify beneficiary details independently before sending funds.

Documents and claims

The parties should agree in writing on required commercial, customs, origin, inspection and quality documents before order confirmation. Certifications or test reports are provided only when available and verified for the relevant lot.
